# Doctolib

**Doctolib,** from booking app to health companion.

Client: Doctolib · Year: 2026 · Role: Lead Product Designer
Services: Vision, Product Design, UX/UI, Visual Identity, Design System

## 2. Statement

Millions of people use Doctolib to book an appointment, then leave. The brief: turn a transactional booking app into a health companion people return to.

## 4. Statement

We started with a week in Paris, immersed in Doctolib's business problems and the health landscape. We benchmarked the space, listened to stakeholders, and played back quick vision ideas and mood boards built on what we'd learnt. Three weeks in, that became a prototype of nine journeys and 86 screens for the CEO.

## 17. Statement

## 21. Statement

Home, the care plan and the AI assistant, taken from vision to detailed UX and UI. Booking was reskinned on the same system as a stress test.

## 26. Statement

The visual identity we created for the product vision held up when it scaled into a full brand. Doctolib is entering a new era, in how people manage their health and how professionals give care. The brand needed convictions broad enough to guide both: simple, honest and flexible.

## 27. Statement

We're honing the art of clinical science to help people and practitioners create *more healthy years in every life*, through our presence making action simple.
